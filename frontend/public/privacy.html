Tasklore

Privacy

Tasklore is a prototype built for the Google Cloud AI Builder Cup 2026. Last updated 16 October 2026.

The public demo is shared

Everyone who signs in to the demo works in the same sample workspace and can see what others add there. Please don't enter real company information or anyone's personal details.

What Tasklore stores

  • From Google sign-in: your name, email address and account ID, to know who you are and which role you act in.
  • What you create: playbooks written from recordings, onboarding plans and progress, the notes you type or say when stuck (spoken questions are kept as text), recording requests, and a record of what the gap-closing agent did.

What it doesn't keep

  • The videos you record or upload. Gemini reads them to write the playbook; Tasklore keeps only the playbook.
  • Screenshots sent to "Check my work". Only the result of the check is saved.
  • The audio of spoken questions, and anything you show the live coach. The coach session runs between your browser and Gemini.

Who processes it

  • Google Cloud: Cloud Run and Firestore in Mumbai (asia-south1), Firebase Authentication and Firebase Hosting.
  • Google's Gemini API reads recordings, screenshots, audio and text to write playbooks, plans and hints, under the Gemini API terms. Videos over 15 MB go through Gemini's file storage, which deletes them after 48 hours.
  • The gap-closing agent never sees email addresses: people and tasks reach it only as labels.

No ads and no analytics trackers. Google Cloud keeps its standard request logs (such as IP address and time) for running the service. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.
